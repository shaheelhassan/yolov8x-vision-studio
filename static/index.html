YOLOv8x is the flagship, largest, and most accurate deep learning model in the YOLOv8 family developed by Ultralytics. It features ~68 million parameters with high-resolution multi-scale feature pyramids (PANet) and decoupled anchor-free detection heads, achieving a state-of-the-art 53.9 mAP on COCO (50-95).
